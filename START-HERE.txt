CLASSICAL CHESS — KINDLE COLORSOFT EDITION

1. Upload the whole classical-chess folder to your website.
2. Keep index.html, styles.css, app.js, engine.js and the assets folder together.
3. Open the website address on your Kindle Colorsoft using its Web Browser.
4. Tap a piece, then tap its highlighted destination square.

White moves first. No dragging, animations, sound, external libraries, or online opponent are used.
